07 THE DEVELOPMENT VISION

A thoughtful plan.
A lasting community.

Everhaven is being shaped as a potential senior living destination in Central Florida. The work ahead is disciplined: validate the market, identify a suitable site, confirm the operating model, secure capital, and complete the approvals required before construction or operations.

PHASE 01 / VALIDATEMarket & feasibility

Study local demand, demographics, competing communities, service needs, and project economics with qualified advisors.

PHASE 02 / SITEProperty & due diligence

Screen sites for zoning, access, utilities, environmental conditions, infrastructure, and development suitability.

PHASE 03 / STRUCTUREDesign, team & approvals

Define the concept, engage experienced design and operating professionals, and pursue required land-use and regulatory approvals.

PHASE 04 / CAPITALFinancing & partnerships

Build a documented capital plan, evaluate appropriate financing and partnership structures, and complete investor diligence.

PHASE 05 / DELIVERConstruction & readiness

After approvals and financing, plan construction, staffing, operating systems, inspections, and required licensing.

This is a proposed roadmap, not a report of completed milestones or a guaranteed schedule. No site, construction budget, opening date, operating partner, or financing commitment is represented as secured.

08 PROPERTY ACQUISITION STRATEGY

The right place
comes first.

Potential Central Florida properties will be screened against practical development criteria. A site advances only after professional review confirms its land use and infrastructure can support the selected community model.

01Land use & zoning

Compatible land-use approvals and a clear entitlement path.

02Access & location

Practical access for residents, families, employees, and emergency services.

03Utilities & infrastructure

Documented capacity for water, sewer, power, stormwater, roads, and communications.

04Environmental diligence

Survey, wetlands, flood risk, geotechnical, environmental, and title review.

05Market fit

Evidence of local demand, competition, and a viable service-area strategy.

06Financial discipline

Independent valuation, realistic site and development costs, and sensitivity analysis.
